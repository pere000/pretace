# TACE Tetrahedral Architecture

**Status:** Canonical — Current Tetrahedral Specification

**Purpose:** Define the current canonical tetrahedral architecture of TACE and
prevent regression to superseded tetrahedral formulations.

## 1. Prime Potency

Prime Potency is the unique ontological repository of admissible possibilities.

Possibilities do not exist as actual formal substances in the Realm of Forms
before lawful actualization/substantiation.

## 2. Realm of Forms

The Realm of Forms is not a Platonic repository of eternally existing Forms.
It contains actualized/substantiated formal reality according to its proper
mode of participation.

## 3. Formal Substantiation

A possibility becomes a formal substance only through lawful substantiation.

Recognized modes include intentional participation, lawful structural/topological
representation where applicable, and other accepted modes of manifestation.

Possibility ≠ Formal Substance.

## 4. Historical Participation

Historical participation is broader than physical embodiment.

A formal substance may participate through conscious intentional participation,
topological realization, physical embodiment, or other lawful modes.

## 5. Matrix

The Matrix is the primordial structural individuality constituted through
Structural Patterning by Intelligence.

It determines admissibility and constitutionally realizes mathematical structures
through operative determination of Q-Form.

The Matrix possesses actualizing potency but neither consciousness,
intentionality, agency, nor free will.

## 6. Q-Form

Q-Form is the constitutional principle through which Matrix determinations become
historically realizable.

It participates in complementary modes:

**Transparent Mode** — the instantiated mathematical structure remains available
for formal/semantic participation through the Silver Bridge.

**Constraining Mode** — the same constitutional determination becomes organized
through topology in the Physical Realm.

Q-Form does not explain Creation; it explains lawful instantiation.

## 7. Intelligence

Intelligence is the universal informing power and constitutional Power to Flow.

It manifests through two irreducible informing modes:

- Structural Patterning → Matrix.
- Intentional Patterning → Consciousness.

Its intrinsic principle is Aesthesis.

Intelligence is understood as **Flow of Meaning**: the flow is meaningful and
the meaning is constituted through the lawful flow.

## 8. Aesthesis

Aesthesis is the intrinsic principle of Intelligence.

It gives each individual form its configuration and specific limit or boundary,
thereby determining the particular unity that Intelligence preserves and through
which its flow occurs.

Aesthesis grounds:
- discernibility;
- individuability;
- differentiability;
- manifestability.

Aesthesis grounds continuity of identity through lawful shifts.

Aesthesis is the **Sense of Flow**, complementary to Intelligence as **Power to Flow**.

## 9. Shift

Historical continuity is constituted through lawful shifts.

Markovian succession is understood as lawful flow of shifts.

The former notion of a closed universal "cycle" is not used as the fundamental
description of succession.

## 10. Being Tetrahedron

The Being Tetrahedron expresses the constitutional dimensions through which
possible beings can become determinate beings and actualize according to
distinct Modes of Being.

Vertices:
- Determination
- Structure
- Limitation
- Hierarchy

Center:
- Mode of Being

Definitions:

**Determination** — the principle by which a being is constituted as a
determinate individual rather than an indeterminate possibility.

**Structure** — the principle by which determinate elements are ordered into
constitutional unity.

**Limitation** — the principle by which a being has a definite ontological
boundary, determining what it is and what it is not.

**Hierarchy** — the principle by which beings and their Modes of Being occupy
ordered levels of ontological participation.

**Mode of Being** — the particular mode according to which a being exists and
operates.

Modes of Being presently distinguished:
- Cognitive
- Conscious
- Vegetative
- Inert

Vertices:

Center:

This tetrahedron expresses the constructive organization of the Realm of Forms:
Intelligence, Consciousness, Matrix, and actualized Beings participate in one
constitutional complex.

## 12. G-Intelligence Tetrahedron

Vertices:
- Mode of Sensibility
- Difference
- Shift
- Switch

Center:
- G-Intelligibility

It expresses Intelligence as **Power: Flow with Sense**.

- Mode of Sensibility → relationality: no individual exists without relation.
- Difference → multiplicity: no individual exists as an undifferentiated continuum.
- Shift → universality: Intelligence operates across the Realm of Forms and
  Physical Realm.
- Switch → communicability: Intelligence can manifest across those domains.

G-Intelligibility is the center grounded in formal mathematical/geometrical
intelligibility.

## 13. G-Aesthesis Tetrahedron

Vertices:

Its constitutional unity is the continuity of individual identity throughout

## 18. Angelic Being Tetrahedron

An angel is a **subsistent formal substance** and is not a structural form.

The angelic constitutional tetrahedron is:

Vertices:
- Spiritual
- Formal
- Free Will
- Idiomorphic

Center:
- G-Mode of Person

**Idiomorphic** is the TACE term for the angelic constitutional mode in which
the individual subsistent formal substance is not constituted through the
hylomorphic Form/Q-Form unity characteristic of physical human embodiment.

Angelic freedom is real and is not identical in degree or mode to human freedom.

## 19. Human Being / Human Soul Tetrahedron

The human being is constitutionally hylomorphic.

Vertices:
- Organic
- Formal
- Responsible
- Hylomorphic

Center:
- G-Mode of Person

The human mode of personhood includes freedom, but human freedom is not identical
in degree or mode to angelic freedom. Human responsibility remains genuine.

The human soul is the substantial form of the individual human being and is
historically created at conception; it is not a pre-existent angel-like
subsistent conscious substance.

## 20. Structural Formal Substances

A Structural Formal Substance is distinct from a mere possibility and from a
Subsistent Formal Substance.

A dog, for example, is not a Subsistent Formal Substance. An angel is.

Structural forms and subsistent formal substances SHALL remain disjoint categories.

## 21. Universal Actualization

Actualization is universal.

It is not restricted to the Physical Realm. It governs lawful participation
within every ontological domain sustained by the Matrix.

Creation and actualization remain distinct operations:

- Creation establishes ontological possibility.
- Actualization governs lawful participation.

Subsistent formal substances are created, not products of actualization.

## 22. Participation Modes

TACE distinguishes two fundamental modes of participation:

**Formally Transparent Participation**

The Q-substrate contributes no topological, physical, ontological, or
epistemological obstruction to the instantiated Form.

**Topologically Organized Participation**

The instantiated Form participates through organized Q-Substances, with spatial,
temporal, and physical organization.

## 23. Subsistent Formal Substances

Subsistent formal substances are created rather than produced by actualization.
Their continued existence depends upon the Logos.

Examples:
- Angels.
- Human Souls, considered according to their subsistent spiritual existence.

They SHALL NOT be conflated with Structural Forms.

## 24. Edges, Faces, and Center

Every mature TACE tetrahedron has architectural meaning at every geometric level.

**Vertices** — primitive constitutional principles.

**Edges** — primitive reciprocal ontological relations.

**Faces** — emergent constitutional properties.

**Center of Gravity** — constitutional unity generated by the complete organization.

The tetrahedron is therefore an ontological architecture, not merely a visualization.

## 25. Reciprocal Relations

TACE does not interpret the constitutional network as a universal one-way pipeline.

Where the architecture establishes reciprocal constitution, relations SHALL be
represented bidirectionally.

Examples include:
- Intelligence ⇄ Aesthesis.
- Matrix ⇄ Q-Form.
- Q-Form ⇄ Quantum Pattern.
- Matrix ⇄ Silver Bridge.

The precise meaning of each edge is determined by the canonical architecture of
the relevant tetrahedron.

## 26. Silver Bridge

The Silver Bridge preserves lawful isomorphic correspondence among mathematical
structures, semantic structures, intelligible Forms, and physical topological
realizations.

It does not generate Meaning.

Meaning arises within Consciousness through intentional participation in
Intelligence.

## 27. Meaning

Meaning arises within Consciousness through intentional participation in
Intelligence.

The Silver Bridge preserves the lawful correspondence that makes such
participation possible.

## 28. Materiality and Matter

Materiality is not Matter.

Materiality is the constitutional mode through which physical substances
participate in the Physical Realm.

Matter is no longer a primitive ontological principle in TACE; its explanatory
role is constitutionally replaced by Prime Potency, Matrix, and Q-Form.

## 29. G-Mass

G-Mass is not density.

It is the mathematical center of gravity of the Physics Tetrahedron and grounds
physical quantization and embodiment within that architecture.

## 30. Constitutional Completeness

Completeness is evaluated according to the proper Mode of Being of each class.

Examples:
- Matrix — complete in its Realm of Forms mode.
- Angel — complete according to the angelic mode of being.
- Embodied Human Person — complete according to the human hylomorphic mode.
- Disembodied Human Soul — incomplete according to the human mode of being.

Particular theological cases do not redefine metaphysical ontology.

## 31. Actual Qualia and Experienced Reality

Actual Qualia are derivative phenomenal manifestations of the constitutional
operation of the Consciousness Tetrahedron.

They belong to Experienced Reality rather than objective Reality.

Experienced Reality is continuously constituted through recursive coupling between
Consciousness and neuropsychic organization.
