# TACE Architectural Axioms and Recent Discoveries

**Status:** Living Architectural Document  
**Purpose:** Preserve the stable conceptual discoveries of TACE so that future ontology development remains consistent and does not regress to earlier formulations.

---

# 1. Prime Potency

Prime Potency is the unique ontological repository of admissible possibilities.

Possibilities do **not** exist in the Realm of Forms.

Only potential existence belongs to Prime Potency.

---

# 2. Realm of Forms

The Realm of Forms is **not** a Platonic repository of eternally existing Forms.

It is constituted exclusively by **substantiated formal substances**.

A formal substance exists in the Realm of Forms only after lawful substantiation.

---

# 3. Formal Substantiation

A possibility becomes a formal substance only through lawful substantiation.

Recognized modes presently include:

- Intentional invocation by Consciousness.
- Topological representation (writing, image, icon, sculpture, digital representation, etc.).
- Angelic manifestation (candidate).

Therefore:

Possibility ≠ Formal Substance.

---

# 4. Historical Participation

Historical participation is broader than physical embodiment.

A formal substance may participate historically through:

- Conscious intentional participation.
- Topological realization.
- Physical embodiment.
- Other lawful modes of manifestation.

Physical embodiment is only one particular mode of historical participation.

---

# 5. Matrix

The Matrix is the primordial structural individuality constituted through Structural Patterning by Intelligence.

It does not merely contain admissible structures.

It constitutionally realizes mathematical structures through its operative determination of the Q-Form.

---

# 6. Q-Form

The Q-Form is the constitutional principle through which Matrix determinations become historically realizable.

It possesses two complementary modes.

Transparent Mode

The same mathematical structure remains available for semantic participation through the Silver Bridge.

Constraining Mode

The same mathematical structure becomes topological organization within the Physical Realm.

---

# 7. Intelligence

Intelligence is the universal informing power and the constitutional Power to Flow.

It manifests through two irreducible informing modes.

- Structural Patterning → Matrix
- Intentional Patterning → Consciousness

Its intrinsic principle is Aesthesis.

Intelligence is understood as a **Flow of Meaning**: it is dynamic, and the flow of meaning constitutes the meaning of the flow.

---

# 8. Aesthesis

Aesthesis is the intrinsic principle of Intelligence.

Aesthesis is the principle that confers upon each individual form its configuration and its specific limit or boundary, thereby determining the particular unity that Intelligence must preserve and through which its flow can occur.

Aesthesis grounds:

- discernibility,
- individuability,
- differentiability,
- manifestability.

Aesthesis is responsible for the continuity of identity.

Identity is preserved not across arbitrary change but across the continuous lawful succession in the flow of shifts.

Aesthesis is understood as the **Sense of Flow**: it gives the flow of Intelligence its specific configuration, limit, and continuity of identity.

---

# 9. Shift

Historical continuity is constituted through lawful shifts.

The Markov chain is understood as a lawful flow of shifts.

The previous notion of "cycle" is abandoned.

# 12. G-Intelligence Tetrahedron

Vertices:

- Mode of Sensibility
- Difference
- Shift
- Switch

Center of Gravity:

- G-Intelligibility

The G-Intelligence Tetrahedron expresses Intelligence as **Power: Flow with Sense**.

Its four vertices are understood as follows:

- Mode of Sensibility → relationality: no individual can exist without “the other”.
- Difference → multiplicity: no individual exists as a continuum.
- Shift → universality: Intelligence can operate in the Realm of Forms or in the Physical Realm.
- Switch → communicability: Intelligence can manifest in both the Realm of Forms and the Physical Realm.

G-Intelligibility is the gravitational center of the tetrahedron, grounded in Pure Mathematics or Geometry.

Intelligence is therefore understood as a **Flow of Meaning**.

# 24. Constitutional Completeness

Complete in the Realm of Forms.

Angel

Complete in both the Realm of Forms and the Physical Realm according to the angelic mode of being.

Embodied Human Person
