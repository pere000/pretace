# TACE Philosophical Constitution

**Status:** Canonical

**Authority**

This constitution defines the accepted ontological and metaphysical commitments
of TACE (Tegmark–Aquinas Conceptual Ecosystem). It is subordinate only to
subsequently accepted ontology and accepted ADRs.

## 1. Purpose

1.1. This constitution SHALL define the canonical ontological commitments of TACE.

1.2. It SHALL answer what is true according to TACE at the level of ontology.

1.3. It SHALL remain implementation-independent.

## 2. Scope

2.1. This constitution SHALL concern ontology and metaphysics.

2.2. It SHALL NOT contain software architecture, semantic reasoning rules,
repository organization, or implementation procedures.

2.3. It SHALL use accepted TACE ontology and accepted ADRs.

## 3. Canonical Ontological Commitments

TACE SHALL affirm:

- Logos.
- Creation.
- Prime Potency.
- Matrix.
- λ (Q-Form).
- Forms.
- Actualization.
- Participation.
- Silver Bridge.
- Consciousness.
- Human Soul.
- Angels.
- Reality.
- Whole.
- Universal Actualization.
- Formally Transparent Participation.
- Topologically Organized Participation.
- Formal Transparency.
- Ontological Transparency.
- Epistemological Transparency.
- Subsistent Formal Substances.
- Structural Forms.
- Kantian Noumenon as ontological reference.

The Silver Bridge SHALL be understood as the Universal Onto-Epistemological
Isomorphic Principle through which the Realm of Forms and Physical Realm remain
intrinsically and inextricably interlocked while preserving lawful,
non-reductive correspondence.

## 4. Foundational Axioms

4.1. The Logos SHALL be the unique uncreated Creator.

4.2. Prime Potency SHALL be the first created possibility.

4.3. Every created possibility SHALL depend upon the Logos.

4.4. The Matrix SHALL be created.

4.5. λ (Q-Form) SHALL be created.

4.6. Creation SHALL precede every created possibility.

4.7. Reality SHALL unfold through historical actualization.

4.8. Ontological superposition SHALL ground quantum superposition.

4.9. Human souls SHALL be created historically at conception.

4.10. Nothing SHALL exist outside the Whole.

4.11. Actualization SHALL be universal and SHALL NOT be restricted to the
Physical Realm.

4.12. Created reality SHALL comprise two complementary and inextricably
interlocked domains: the Realm of Forms and the Physical Realm.

4.13. Creation and Actualization SHALL be ontologically distinct operations.

4.14. TACE SHALL distinguish Primordial Creation, Historical Creation, and
Continuous Actualization.

4.15. Human souls SHALL belong to Historical Creation.

4.16. Angels SHALL belong to Primordial Creation.

## 5. Fundamental Ontological Principles

5.1. Being SHALL be understood through the ordered relation of Creator, creation,
potency, form, and actualization.

5.2. Created being SHALL participate in the Whole.

5.3. Created being SHALL receive intelligibility through form.

5.4. Actualization SHALL be the passage from potency to determinate being.

5.5. Participation SHALL express ordered dependence of created being upon the Logos.

5.6. The Matrix SHALL be a created ontological order.

5.7. λ (Q-Form) SHALL be a created formal order within TACE ontology.

5.8. Domain differentiation SHALL be determined by participation mode.

5.9. Formally Transparent Participation SHALL denote non-obstructive participation
of instantiated Form.

5.10. Topologically Organized Participation SHALL denote participation through
organized Q-Substances.

5.11. Structural Forms SHALL participate through actualization.

5.12. Subsistent Formal Substances and Structural Forms SHALL remain ontologically
distinct categories.

## 6. Canonical Relations Among First Principles

6.1. The Logos SHALL stand as the source of all created being.

6.2. Prime Potency SHALL arise within creation and SHALL NOT be uncreated.

6.3. Forms SHALL be intelligible principles of determination.

6.4. Actualization SHALL realize what is possible in creation.

6.5. Participation SHALL relate creatures to the Whole without collapsing
creature into Creator.

6.6. The Silver Bridge SHALL relate consciousness to the ordered reality of the Whole.

6.7. Ontological superposition SHALL be prior to quantum superposition in the
canonical order of explanation.

6.8. Q-Form SHALL be the universal principle of instantiation.

6.9. Q-Form SHALL NOT be identified with Creation.

6.10. The Silver Bridge SHALL constitute the first-principle onto-epistemological
isomorphic relation through which both domains remain lawfully interlocked within
one created Whole.

## 7. Anthropological Commitments

7.1. The Human Soul SHALL be created.

7.2. The Human Soul SHALL be historically created at conception.

7.3. Consciousness SHALL be a real ontological datum in TACE.

7.4. The human being SHALL be ordered through participation in reality and the Whole.

7.5. The Silver Bridge SHALL articulate the relation of consciousness to the
ontological order.

7.6. At conception, creation of the individual human soul and historical emergence
of embodied human participation SHALL be treated as one ontological beginning
of the human being.

## 8. Angelic Commitments

8.1. Angels SHALL be real created beings.

8.2. Angels SHALL NOT be uncreated.

8.3. Angels SHALL belong to the created order while differing from human beings
in mode of being.

8.4. Angels SHALL participate in the Whole according to their proper created nature.

8.5. Angels SHALL belong to Primordial Creation.

8.6. Angels and Human Souls SHALL NOT be conflated as the same ontological mode.

## 9. Reality and the Whole

9.1. Reality SHALL be ordered as a Whole.

9.2. The Whole SHALL include all that exists.

9.3. Nothing SHALL exist outside the Whole.

9.4. Created beings SHALL be intelligible only within the Whole.

9.5. The canonical order of reality SHALL preserve distinction between Creator
and creation.

9.6. The Kantian Noumenon SHALL NOT be treated as a separate ontological realm.

9.7. The Kantian Noumenon SHALL NOT be treated as actualized ontology.

9.8. Ultimate noumenal constitution SHALL remain undetermined within this constitution.

## 10. Canonical Acceptance of TACE Architectural Revisions

TACE canonical acceptance = explicit acceptance by the TACE author/maintainer.

A proposed TACE architectural change becomes canonical when explicitly accepted
by the TACE author/maintainer.

Once accepted, it SHALL be treated as superseding any previous formulation that
it explicitly replaces.

This rule concerns the internal canonical architecture and conceptual ontology
of TACE. It does not constitute ecclesial, magisterial, or external doctrinal
authority.

An explicitly accepted TACE architectural revision SHALL therefore be treated
as canonical for subsequent TACE reasoning even when older documents preserve
the superseded formulation for historical or provenance purposes.
