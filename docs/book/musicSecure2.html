Notes:

Upon loading the page, the first song from the database will play automatically; if the database is empty, the "radio stream" (http://radio.streemlion.com:2740/stream) will play.
Playlists can be in CSV or m3u format. A single entry allows for the inclusion of radio station streams, multiple individual songs from YouTube or other servers, and even other playlists. If asingle entry fails (song, radio station, playlist) the next in that list will play.
In the primary player, a record of a playlist can correspond to a radio station; typically, the broadcasts are continuous, requiring the user to switch stations manually. Therefore, if a radio station is added to a secondary playlist, playback will not normally advance to the subsequent entries while that station is playing.
In the primary player, a playlist entry may correspond to a YouTube playlist; in that case, the playlist loads in the secondary player (it can be exported by clicking the "EXPORT PLAYLIST" button). Generally, YouTube playlists do not function like radio station playlists: when one ends, the next song from the primary playlist plays.
When you click the "Load" button while a "CSV List" is playing, the list is cleared, and only the song, the stream, or the compact audio that was currently playing continues to play. The song-stream-compact will repeat.
