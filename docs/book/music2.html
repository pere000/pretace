Notes:
Upon loading the page, the first song from the database will play automatically; if the database is empty, the "radio stream" (http://radio.streemlion.com:2740/stream) will play. You can load a file of a single song or a playlist by the 'LOAD' button after pressing the 'STOP' button; press the 'Play' button to play the url shown in the 'Url/file' field.
Playlists can be in CSV or m3u format. A CSV/m3u playlist allows single entries of radio station streams, multiple individual songs from YouTube or other sources, and even other 'url playlists'. If a single entry fails (song, radio station, playlist) the next in that list will play.
  • "AUTOPLAY AFTER LOAD" checkbox marked will force the playlist to repeat after finishing.
    • In the primary player, a record of a playlist can correspond to a radio station; typically, the broadcasts are continuous, requiring the user to switch stations manually. Therefore, if a radio station is added to a secondary playlist, playback will not normally advance to the subsequent entries while that station is playing.
    In the primary player, a playlist entry may correspond to a YouTube playlist; in that case, the playlist loads in the secondary player (it can be exported by clicking the "EXPORT PLAYLIST" button). Generally, YouTube playlists do not function like radio station playlists: when one ends, the next song from the primary playlist plays.
    When you click the "Load" button while a "CSV List" is playing, the list is cleared, and only the song, the stream, or the compact audio that was currently playing continue playing.

    Tetrahedra playlists

    A "tetrahedron-type" playlist is a CSV or m3u file containing a list of exactly five entries. The first four correspond to the tetrahedron itself, while the fifth corresponds to its center of gravity. Each entry can be an individual song or a playlist URL; the fifth can act as a connector between tetrahedra. This allows tetrahedra to be chained together, facilitating the management of long chains, transitions between different elements, and shared exchanges.
