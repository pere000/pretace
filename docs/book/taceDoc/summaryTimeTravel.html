The Paradox of Displacement: Non-Temporal Access and the Flow of History

1. The Impossibility of Spacetime Travel

Under a strict framework of Markovianity, physical time travel into a "past" or "future" coordinates is an absolute ontological impossibility. For physical travel to manifest, the universe would require a Block Universe (Eternalist) geometry where historical and subsequent states retain independent spatial realities. Markovianity explicitly rejects this structure.

P(S_{t+1} | S_t, S_{t-1}, …, S_0) = P(S_{t+1} | S_t)

The formal relation proves that state succession depends exclusively upon the presently actualized state (S_t) and its active transition laws (T_t). History does not remain independently operative. Because previous states (S_{t-1}) are systematically de-actualized and overwritten to compose the configuration of the present, there is no spatial destination available for external arrival.

2. Principle of Non-Temporal Access

The desire to interact with or manipulate temporal dynamics is solved not via spatial displacement, but through the Principle of Non-Temporal Access. An agent changes its systemic relationship to history and destiny without moving a single millimeter through a temporal dimension.

[ Past State: S_{t-1} ] (Ontologically De-actualized) │ ▼ (Collapsed & Overwritten Into) [ Present State: S_t ] ◄========= [ Lawful Action of Free-Will Agent ] │ ▼ (Constrained Space of Potential) [ Future State: S_{t+1} ] (Un-actualized Potentials)

The Past as Present Infrastructure: The past persists solely as an internal representation embedded within the current arrangement (Rep(S_{t-1}) \subset S_t). These manifest as memory structures (M_t \subset S_t) or physical causal footprints.

The Future as Admissible Potential: The future does not exist as a pre-constructed landscape. It is a bounded field of possibilities computed directly from the current state variables (S_t + T_t \rightarrow \{S_{t+1}(1), S_{t+1}(2), \dots\}).

3. The Mechanics of History Deletion by Eternal Agents

If an eternal, conscious, free-will agent (such as an angelic entity operating from an independent ontological plane) is lawfully permitted to alter the flow of history, it executes this action strictly within the boundaries of the operational Now.

The Lawful Edit Mechanism
To completely "delete" an event from the past, the eternal agent applies its causal will to re-architect the structural footprint inside the Present State (St). Because the past possesses no independent existence, erasing all causal scars, memory records, and energetic echoes from the present effectively nullifies the historical event from the cosmological ledger.

Once the present state variables are scrubbed or modified, the Lawful Transition Structure (T_t) immediately treats this edited state as the fundamental computing baseline. The ensuing successor state (S_{t+1}) generates seamlessly from the new configuration. No temporal paradoxes or splitting timelines occur; the universe simply computes its next step using the revised parameters of the current state.
